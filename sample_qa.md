**Q: What is the exit load of Axis Flexi Cap Fund?**

**A:** If redeemed or switched-out within 3 months from the date of allotment, the exit load is NIL for 10% of investments and 1% for the remaining investments. If redeemed or switched-out after 3 months from the date of allotment, the exit load is NIL.
